Root asset tree for production-ready image organization.

brand/logos: site and product logos
brand/social: social network icons and badges
ui/arrows: directional arrows and navigation affordances
ui/icons: shared UI icons
ui/patterns: folk patterns and ornamental assets
ui/textures: background textures and decorative surfaces
sports/<sport>/heroes: page hero images
sports/<sport>/gameplay: action photography
sports/<sport>/teams: team and roster photography
sports/<sport>/equipment: sticks, balls, and gear
organizations/sponsors: sponsor logos
organizations/partners: partner logos
organizations/schools: school crests and team institutions
locations/maps: map screenshots and location visuals
content/news: editorial and news-specific imagery that is not tied to a single sport taxonomy
reference/figma-frames: exported design frames for reference only
reference/raw-exports: uncategorized intake before sorting
placeholders: fallback images and temporary stand-ins
